# KIRO — Final Architecture Reconciliation Pass

Read the complete `DSX_Kiro_Master_Implementation_Blueprint.md`, the original `DSX-project-plan.md`, and the existing architecture documentation.

Your task is to reconcile their contradictions and produce the final authoritative specification for five parallel development workstreams.

The new blueprint is the preferred technical starting point, but mathematical consistency and explicit human-approved decisions take precedence over reproducing it verbatim.

## Mandatory corrections

1. **Patient accounting:** Separate incident infections, new hospital admissions, existing hospital occupancy, scheduled discharges, and unmet demand. Rerouting must operate on new admissions only for MVP. Do not repeatedly allocate existing inpatients.

2. **Weekly state transitions:** Specify exactly when interventions activate, discharges occur, demand is generated, provisional assignments are evaluated, final admissions are committed, and FDNA scores are recorded. Use a persistent `HospitalState` and immutable weekly snapshots.

3. **Two-pass FDNA evaluation:** Allow a pre-rerouting evaluation and a post-rerouting evaluation, but do not commit provisional patient assignments or introduce unbounded FDNA/capacity feedback loops.

4. **Graph semantics:** Maintain separate accessibility, patient-flow, and FDNA dependency edges. Geographic proximity does not prove functional dependence. Symmetric hospital dependencies must be configurable assumptions, not asserted facts.

5. **Rerouting:** Define global competition between districts for available beds. Preserve originating district IDs, maintain capacity constraints, and ensure `new_admissions = accepted + unmet`. Redirected admissions are a subset of accepted admissions.

6. **SEIR conservation:** Explicitly define whether `H` belongs to the mutually exclusive epidemic compartments or is an auxiliary metric, and adapt the population conservation equation accordingly.

7. **Scenario isolation:** Scenarios must start from identical initial states and random seeds. No cross-scenario mutations. Closing a hospital does not automatically erase existing inpatients.

8. **Synthetic data:** Synthetic recovery experiments must not be described as external validation. Historical real-world backtests are optional and only reported after actual computation.

9. **Scope:** Keep the weekly Python/Streamlit nine-block pipeline. Do not introduce the alternative daily ICU/mortality-feedback architecture, React/FastAPI, Jev, or LLM layers in the MVP.

10. **Canonical interfaces:** Every cross-module object must have one definition, explicit units, invariants, serialization conventions, and an owning workstream.
